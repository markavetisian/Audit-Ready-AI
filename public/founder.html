Vlad Avetisian
Founder, Audit Ready AI

This is the road from building websites to building trust.

2020Where it started

I started building websites and working in marketing. In the early days, I focused on creating websites, testing ideas, and understanding how businesses attract users and grow online.

2021The AI shift

When large language models started becoming popular in 2021, I was one of the early people to experience what later became known as "vibe coding." I saw how AI was changing the way software could be built, letting individuals move faster, experiment more, and create things that used to require much larger teams.

I spent that time building websites, simple products, AI-powered tools, and automation systems while learning how the entire software development process was changing.

2024–2025Going deeper

My focus moved from websites into deeper technical systems. I started working on infrastructure, AI agents, software platforms, and tools that solved more complex problems.

While building these systems, I noticed a problem that kept appearing.

Companies could build and launch products faster than ever, but becoming enterprise-ready was still slow and complicated.

Security reviews, compliance requirements, and preparation for SOC 2 often became a major obstacle for growing startups.

TodayAudit Ready AI

That is why I built Audit Ready AI. It helps startups understand their security readiness, identify gaps, and prepare for SOC 2 requirements with a faster and more organized process.

The goal is simple: make it easier for companies to move from building products to becoming trusted businesses.
